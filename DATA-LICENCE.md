## The derived data tables: ODbL 1.0

The tables in `outputs/` (travel times, road distances, and everything computed from them: cost, emissions,
accessibility, the county commute used in the enrolment model) are derived from OpenStreetMap and are made available
under the Open Database Licence 1.0 (https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). Any rights in individual
contents are licensed under the Database Contents Licence 1.0. If you publicly use a database adapted from them, you
must offer it under the ODbL as well.

The CC BY 4.0 sources combined into these tables ask only for attribution, which is given below and kept by the ODbL
release.

## The inputs in data/raw/: their publishers' licences

Each file keeps its publisher's licence, all CC BY 4.0. `data/raw/MANIFEST.md` lists every input (including the large
ones not stored here) with its source, date and checksum. The Department of Education schools file is redistributed
with the principal-name, email and phone columns removed; nothing else is changed.

Not redistributed: the OpenStreetMap extract (download it by date from Geofabrik), and the CAO course list, which has
no open licence (the report shows a few rows, cited).
